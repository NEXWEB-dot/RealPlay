Print checklist
  • Print at 100 % / actual size — never "fit to page" (that changes the size).
  • Check the ruler below against a real ruler; if it's off, measure the printed black square and enter that size in the game's Tracking & camera screen.
  • Thick, matte, flat surface (no glare, no folds). The white 1-unit quiet zone around each black square is part of the marker — keep it clear.
  • Lamp the play area evenly; avoid pointing a light straight at the marker.
